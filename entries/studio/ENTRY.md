## About this cut
The letter is written on the wall as Leonardo dictates it: bridges, moats, cannons, covered carts and more for war, then buildings and water for peace, and painting last, in red. Borges brackets the nine war offers, and the camera circles the word *painting* when he calls him one of the greatest painters who ever lived. "Who pays the painter now?" is the last line on the page.
