FUTURE RADIO: THE LETTER (a one-minute sample)
Characters: Leonardo, Borges, Agnolo
Setting: Future Radio studio. A letter on the wall.
BORGES: Milan. Around fourteen eighty-two. A young man writes to a duke.
LEONARDO: Most illustrious lord. I can build bridges, light and strong, and easy to carry.
AGNOLO: He needs money.
LEONARDO: I can drain the moats of a city under siege. I can make cannons, and covered carts that no army can break.
AGNOLO: He needs a lot of money.
LEONARDO: And in time of peace, I can raise buildings, and bring water from one place to another.
BORGES: Ten promises. Nine of them are for war. Because war is what dukes pay for.
LEONARDO: And also, I can paint.
AGNOLO: Near the end?
LEONARDO: Near the end.
BORGES: One of the greatest painters who ever lived put painting near the bottom of the page.
AGNOLO: Because nobody paid for painting.
LEONARDO: So. Who pays the painter now?
End.
