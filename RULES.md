# Rules

## What's fixed
- **The soundtrack.** Use `kit/the-letter-sample-60s-audio.wav` unchanged: no re-timing, cuts, extra music, sound effects or re-recorded voices. The sound is the same for everyone, so only the pictures are judged.
- **The length.** Exactly 60 seconds, starting at 0:00 of the soundtrack. A few frames of black or a title card are fine if they stay inside the 60 seconds.
- **The shape.** 16:9, at least 1280×720. A vertical cut is welcome too, but submit the 16:9 one as your entry.

## What's free
- Style, medium, tools and character designs. Hand-drawn, stop-motion, 3D, puppets, live action, code, AI or any mix.
- Your reading of the scene. Literal, abstract, funny or serious.
- Subtitles: up to you.

## Fair play
- **One entry per person or team.** You can replace it with a new version until submissions close.
- **Say what you used.** The entry form asks for your tools, including any AI. AI entries are allowed; hiding how you made it is not.
- **Original pictures.** Don't use other people's characters, logos or artwork without the right to.
- **The studio's cut is locked.** It was finished and published on 2026-11-17, before anyone else started, and it won't be changed after seeing other entries.

## Rights
- You keep the rights to your pictures.
- By entering, you let Future Radio show your entry side by side with the others in public posts, streams and episodes about the battle, credited to you. Ask and we'll take it down.
- The kit (soundtrack, script, cue sheet, studio cut) is licensed **CC BY 4.0** so you can build on it. Credit: "The Letter, Future Radio".

## Voting
- Entries go up side by side with the studio's cut, and anyone can vote for free.
- The voting format and dates will be announced in this repo and on [@leo_guinan](https://x.com/leo_guinan) before voting opens. Until then, nothing here promises a prize or a particular voting method.
